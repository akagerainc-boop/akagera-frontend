2Introduction

BPay – Easy Mobile Payments ("BPay", "we", "our", or "us") is a mobile application developed and operated by Akagera Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, protected, and disclosed when you use the BPay application.

BPay helps you pay faster. You enter or pick a recipient phone number or merchant code, choose an amount (or tap a quick amount), and BPay starts the right USSD code for your MTN or Airtel SIM. You can also buy services such as airtime or bill payments, view your payment history and spending summary, create shareable payment links, and lock the app with a PIN or your fingerprint.

This policy covers the BPay app and the BPay server that supports it. It does not cover the general Akagera Inc website. The website has its own website privacy policy.

3Information we collect

BPay has no user accounts, passwords, or sign-in. We process only the information below.

3.1 Your own phone number(s)

During setup you can add your own mobile number, and optionally a second one, so BPay knows which SIM and network you are paying from. On Android, BPay can offer Google's Phone Number Hint picker to fill this in. You choose the number in a Google Play services screen, and BPay receives only the number you pick. Your numbers are stored on your device. The number you are paying from is included when BPay reports a transaction or starts a service-fee request to our server (see 3.3 and 3.6).

3.2 Payment details you enter

To start a payment, you provide:

  • the recipient's phone number or a merchant code (typed, picked from your contacts, picked from recent recipients, or scanned from a photo);
  • the amount;
  • for bills and other services, the account, meter, or reference number the service asks for (and, for some services, a national ID number when the operator requires one);
  • which SIM or network you are paying from.

BPay puts these details into the USSD code that your phone then sends to your operator. BPay never asks for, sees, or stores your mobile-money PIN. You always type your PIN into your operator's own USSD screen.

3.3 Transaction records

BPay keeps a history of your payments on your device, so you can see recent payments, receipts, and your spending summary. It also sends a report of each payment attempt to the BPay server. Each report contains:

  • a BPay transaction ID and the date and time;
  • the random app install ID (see 3.4) and the phone number you paid from;
  • the network (MTN or Airtel) and the type of transaction;
  • the recipient number or merchant code, and the recipient's name if one is shown in the app (for example, a name taken from your contacts);
  • the amount;
  • the result (for example, successful, failed, or unconfirmed), how that result was determined, the operator's transaction reference if there is one, and the status message shown to you, which may include text from your operator's reply;
  • the payment link code, if you paid through someone's BPay payment link.

BPay treats these reports as your app's own record, not as proof that a payment happened. Your operator's own records remain the official record.

3.4 App install ID and notification token

On first launch, BPay creates a random ID for that installation. This ID is not your IMEI, serial number, or advertising ID. BPay also receives a push-notification token from Firebase Cloud Messaging. BPay sends the install ID, the token, and your platform (Android) to our server, so we can send you announcements and transaction alerts. BPay also reads its own app version to check whether an update is required.

3.5 Payment links

If you create a payment link, our server stores the link code, your install ID, your phone number and network, the destination number or merchant code, the amount, the link's status, and how many payments were received through it.

3.6 Service fees

Where a BPay service fee applies (for example, a payment-link usage fee), BPay shows you the fee first. If you agree, it sends your phone number, network, the amount, and your install ID to our server. Our server then sends a "request to pay" to your mobile-money provider, which you approve on your phone. We keep a record of each fee request and its result.

3.7 Support messages

If you email or call us, we receive the information you give us, such as your email address, phone number, and your message.

What BPay does not collect: your mobile-money PIN or balance, passwords, location, SMS messages, call logs, photos from your gallery, your fingerprint or face data, or an advertising ID. BPay contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

5Accessibility Service disclosure

BPay includes an optional Android Accessibility Service. It is off by default. It runs only if you turn it on yourself in Android Settings → Accessibility → BPay, after BPay explains what it does.

What it does: after a USSD payment, your operator shows its reply in a system dialog (for example, "You have sent 5,000 RWF to …" or "Insufficient balance"). The service reads the text of that dialog, so BPay can tell whether the payment succeeded, failed, or is unclear, and can record the result and any transaction reference.

What it does not do: it reads only on-screen text from dialog windows. It does not read your typing, passwords, or PIN, and it does not perform any taps or actions for you. Your PIN is typed into your operator's own screen, never into BPay.

Where the data goes: the reply text is used on your device to classify the result. To classify it more accurately, BPay may send the reply text to Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic (see Section 10). If that is unavailable, BPay classifies the text on your device. The resulting status, reference, and message become part of the transaction record described in 3.3.

You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility. BPay will keep working, but you may be asked to confirm payment results yourself.

6Biometric and PIN security

BPay may use your device's biometric authentication feature, such as fingerprint authentication, to help protect access to the application. Biometric verification is handled by the operating system and BPay does not receive or store your actual fingerprint or biometric template. BPay receives only a yes or no result from Android.

You can also set a 4-digit BPay App Lock PIN. This PIN only unlocks the BPay app. It is not your mobile-money PIN. It is never sent to our server. It is stored on your device only as a salted, one-way hash inside Android's secure keystore.

7How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • provide BPay's features, such as starting USSD payments, buying services, and showing history, receipts, and spending summaries;
  • identify payment recipients and merchants, and work out which network a number belongs to;
  • determine and record whether a payment succeeded;
  • create and resolve payment links, and count the payments made through them;
  • show and request any applicable BPay service fee, with your approval;
  • send transaction alerts and service announcements;
  • keep USSD codes and service lists up to date, and tell you when an app update is required;
  • protect BPay and its users, including detecting abuse, fraud, or unauthorised access;
  • understand how BPay is used, measure its reliability, and fix problems;
  • respond to support requests;
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

8Mobile money and USSD

BPay is not a bank, a telecommunications operator, or a mobile-money provider, and it does not hold or move your money. BPay prepares the USSD code for your payment and asks your phone to dial it. Your operator (MTN Rwanda for MTN MoMo, or Airtel Rwanda for Airtel Money) then runs the transaction in its own USSD session, where you confirm it with your own PIN.

BPay may initiate supported USSD or mobile-money payment flows using services provided by third-party telecommunications or mobile-money operators. The processing of transactions by those providers is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

9Information sharing

We share information only in the following cases:

Your mobile-money operator

When you pay, the USSD code, which contains the recipient, amount, and any account or reference number, is sent by your phone to MTN or Airtel so they can carry out the transaction.

Service fee payment processors

When you approve a BPay service fee, your phone number, network, and the amount are sent to the mobile-money collection service that handles the request: MTN MoMo API, Airtel Money API, or ITEC Pay, depending on how the fee is configured.

Service providers

We use providers that process data on our behalf to run BPay, such as Google Firebase and our server hosting provider. They are listed in Section 10.

People who pay through your payment link

If you share a payment link, anyone who opens it can see the destination number or merchant code and the amount you set.

Legal requirements

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law, regulation, court order, or lawful governmental request.

Protection and safety

Information may be processed or disclosed to detect or prevent fraud, abuse, security threats, or unauthorised activity, and to protect the rights and safety of our users, the public, or Akagera Inc.

Business changes

If Akagera Inc is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction and will remain subject to this policy.

11Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • HTTPS (TLS) encryption for communication between the app and the BPay server;
  • an App Lock PIN kept only as a salted hash in the Android keystore, plus optional biometric unlock handled by Android;
  • a design where BPay never handles your mobile-money PIN;
  • credentials for payment providers kept only on our server, never in the app;
  • an administration dashboard that requires authentication.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect your phone with a screen lock and keep BPay updated.

12Data retention

On your device: your phone numbers, settings, recent recipients, and payment history stay on your phone until you remove them in the app, clear BPay's storage, or uninstall BPay.

On our server: we retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements. We do not currently apply a fixed automatic deletion period. When you delete a payment link in the app, it stops working immediately, but its record may be kept for fee and dispute purposes until you ask us to delete your data.

13Account and data deletion

BPay has no user account. The information we hold about you is linked to your phone number(s) and your app install ID.

To request deletion of your BPay data from our server, email akagerainc@gmail.com with the subject "BPay data deletion request". Include the phone number(s) you used in BPay. We may ask you to confirm that you control those numbers, for example by replying from that number or approving a verification step, before we act.

We will then delete the transaction reports, payment links, service-fee records, and notification registrations linked to those numbers and your install ID, unless we must keep some of them to meet a legal obligation, resolve a dispute, or prevent fraud. If so, we will tell you what we kept and why.

To delete data stored on your phone: open Android Settings → Apps → BPay → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall BPay. This removes your saved numbers, history, App Lock PIN, and install ID from the device.

Deleting data held by MTN, Airtel, or a payment processor must be requested from them directly.

14Children's privacy

BPay is intended for people who can lawfully hold and use a mobile-money account, and it is not directed at children. BPay is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child in circumstances where such collection is not permitted, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. If you believe a child has used BPay, please contact us.
